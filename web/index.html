PegLab · Kaspa Testnet-10

A unit for dapps on Kaspa.
Not a bank stablecoin.

Dapps need something stable enough to invoice, escrow, and pay fees. They should not have to wait on Circle or Tether. PegLab is that alternative idea: a covenant unit sequenced on Kaspa, backed by locked tKAS. This page is the teaching build. If you call tPEG a US dollar, it will depeg. We show that on purpose.

The idea

Skip centralized stables for dapps.

USDT/USDC are cash in a company. Fine for a listed dollar. Bad as the only way a Kaspa app can run. PegLab explores a Kaspa-native unit: lock tKAS, issue tPEG, redeem under a spend rule. The dapp talks to Kaspa, not to an issuer.

This lab is cheap on purpose (tiny pool, one admin price). That is why a dollar peg fails here. The takeaway is not “ship this as money.” It is “dapps can settle on Kaspa rules; a fake $1 is not the product.”

How to try it

  1. Stay on this page. Testnet-10 only. No seed goes in the box.
  2. A — Connect KasWare, switch it to Testnet 10, see your tKAS balance.
    B — No wallet? Make local wallet + fund from host (1 tKAS from the host address).
  3. Click Run depeg lab below.
  4. Read three numbers: admin price, pool price, redeem price. They will not match. That is why this is not USDT.
